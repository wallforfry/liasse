The graph keeps its labels at one size on screen and hides those that would overlap another: zooming in spreads the nodes under them and shows more labels, zooming out leaves the larger nodes labelled. The node in focus and its neighbors keep their labels whatever they cover. A search field above the graph draws only the nodes whose label holds the text, case and accents aside, with their neighbors, and labels the matches first.
