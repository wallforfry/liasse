A graph of the archive. `GET /graph` (`graph` of the SDK) answers the most recent documents with their types and parents, the subjects they are about and the entities they name, and the links found between documents, private documents only when asked. The web UI draws it on a Graph page: pan and zoom, hide a kind of node, and select a node to see what it is joined to and open its document or type.
