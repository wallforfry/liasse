Add the knowledge layer (`@liasse/core/knowledge`). `prepareKnowledge` and `persistKnowledge` store the key dates, the facts and the subjects of a document in its write transaction: the notice deadline is computed from the renewal date and the notice period (a calendar subtraction, never the model's date; an invalid period is dropped alone and counted), subjects are resolved like entities (plate, vehicle identification number, IBAN, a contract number of its issuer, or an address with its house number and postal code, then label, then similarity with arbitration) under the lock of the entities, and an IBAN or a card number left in a fact is masked. A private document may attach to a subject but adds nothing to it, and a subject only private documents named is shown to no reader who did not ask for them; a subject no live document concerns is blanked. `mergeSubjects`, `proposeSubjectMerge` and `findSubjectMergeCandidates` merge duplicate subjects through `merge_subject` changes. `detectLinks`, queued as the `detect_links` job, asks the text model once about the candidate documents and writes the links it keeps with both documents locked; `documents.state` follows from the links, a document has one superseder, an amendment follows the state of the contract it amends, and deleting a document gives the documents it superseded or terminated their state back. Merges lock the documents they rewrite in id order. `upcoming`, `timeline`, `recall` and `getSubject` are the logic of the four knowledge tools. `document_links` gains the trace columns (`model`, `quote`, `schema_version`, `page`) and `subjects` a `private` flag: run the migration.
