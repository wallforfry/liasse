Add the knowledge layer (`@liasse/core/knowledge`). `prepareKnowledge` and `persistKnowledge` store the key dates, the facts and the subjects of a document in its write transaction: the notice deadline is computed from the renewal date and the notice period (a calendar subtraction, never the model's date), subjects are resolved like entities (plate, vehicle identification number, IBAN, contract number or full address, then label, then similarity with arbitration) under the lock of the entities, and an IBAN or a card number left in a fact is masked. `mergeSubjects`, `proposeSubjectMerge` and `findSubjectMergeCandidates` merge duplicate subjects through `merge_subject` changes. `detectLinks`, queued as the `detect_links` job, asks the text model once about the candidate documents and writes the links it keeps with both documents locked; `documents.state` follows from the links, and deleting a document gives the documents it superseded or terminated their state back. `upcoming`, `timeline`, `recall` and `getSubject` are the logic of the four knowledge tools. `document_links` gains the trace columns (`model`, `quote`, `schema_version`, `page`): run the migration.
