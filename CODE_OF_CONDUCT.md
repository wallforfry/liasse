# Code of conduct

Liasse wants a community where anyone can ask, contribute and disagree without being harassed or belittled, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, religion or sexual orientation.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Discuss the work: criticise code, designs and decisions, never the person who wrote them.
- Accept a decision you disagree with once it is made, and say so when you were wrong.
- Keep other people's private information private. Liasse is about personal documents: never post someone's document, identifier or address, even as an example.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, and deliberate intimidation, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Discriminatory jokes and language.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This code applies in every space of the project (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, chats) and wherever someone represents the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a breach with GitHub's _Report content_ on the comment concerned, choosing to report it to the repository's maintainers. When no comment is concerned, use the private reporting form of [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) and say that the report is about conduct. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and other contributions, and warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban a contributor, in proportion to the breach and to whether it repeats. A decision is explained to the person concerned.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
